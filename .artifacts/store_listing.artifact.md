## Full Description
Kyron is a free, open-source, local-first restaurant health inspection app designed to give you transparent, offline-capable access to health department scores, inspection history, and detailed violation breakdowns.

### Key Features:
- **100% Local-First:** Works fully offline with bundled regional health data and SQLite FTS search.
- **Detailed Inspection History:** View exact violation codes, critical flags, inspector remarks, and historical scores.
- **Interactive Map & Proximity Filter:** Discover health scores near your current location or across supported counties.
- **Favorites & Alerts:** Save your favorite establishments and receive update alerts when new inspection scores are published.
- **Zero Tracking & No Ads:** Free to use without subscriptions, mandatory user accounts, tracking pixels, or advertisements.

### Data Transparency & Sources:
Kyron sources official public health inspection data directly from participating state and county health departments (such as ADPH, JCDH, MCHD). Kyron is an independent public-interest tool and is not officially endorsed by any government agency.
